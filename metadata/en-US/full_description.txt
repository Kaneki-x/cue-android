Cue is a free, secure and open source 2FA app for Android. It keeps your tokens in an encrypted vault and gets the code to where you need it.

Features:
- A keyboard that inserts the code of any entry into the focused field and switches back to your usual keyboard
- A home screen widget that shows the codes of your favorites, hidden until you tap them
- Encrypted vault (AES-256-GCM) protected by a password and optionally biometrics
- Automatic and manual backups
- Import from Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 2FAS, Authy and many others
- HOTP and TOTP, compatible with thousands of services
- Groups, favorites, search and icon packs
- Material 3 design with dynamic colors
