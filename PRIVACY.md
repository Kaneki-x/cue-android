# Cue 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 18 日 · Last updated: October 18, 2026

Cue 是一款两步验证（验证码）应用，有 Android、iOS 和 macOS 版本，Mac 上另有浏览器扩展和 Cue 输入助手。Cue 不收集你的任何数据。

- **不收集数据**：没有账号，不含广告、统计、崩溃上报或第三方 SDK，不向开发者或任何第三方发送信息。
- **数据只在你的设备上**：验证码数据库保存在你的设备上，设置了密码时用 AES-256-GCM 加密。
- **网络**：Cue for Android 不申请网络权限。Cue for iOS 和 macOS 只在两种情况下联网：开启“iCloud 同步”后，加密的数据库保存在你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库中，只有你的 Apple 账户能访问，开发者无法读取；打开 Cue Pro 页面、购买或恢复购买时，系统会连接 App Store。
- **购买（iOS 和 macOS）**：Cue Pro 通过 App Store 购买，付款由 Apple 处理，开发者收不到你的付款信息或 Apple 账户信息。是否已购买由系统记录在你的设备上，Cue 只在本机读取。
- **相机、照片与屏幕**：只用来读取二维码，画面和图片在本机处理，不保存、不上传。Mac 上只有你选择“从屏幕扫描”时才截取屏幕。
- **备份与导出**：只写到你选择的位置。
- **键盘与自动填充**：Cue 键盘和自动填充只读取 Cue 的数据库来提供验证码，不读取、不记录你输入的内容。
- **浏览器扩展（Mac）**：Chrome、Edge、Firefox 等浏览器里的 Cue 扩展在网页上找出验证码输入框。只有你点 Cue 图标、打开扩展的弹出窗口或按快捷键时，它才把当前网页的域名交给同一台 Mac 上的 Cue（通过浏览器的本机消息通道，不经过网络），再把 Cue 给出的验证码填进输入框。它不读取网页的其他内容，不记录浏览历史，不联网，不保存任何数据。
- **Cue 输入助手（Mac）**：只接收 Cue 发来的验证码，并在 Cue 的面板打开时读取前台 App 的名称、窗口标题、浏览器页面的地址和是否有输入框处于焦点，用来决定把验证码输入到哪里。这些信息只交给同一台 Mac 上的 Cue，不保存、不上传。
- **删除数据**：卸载 Cue 会删除设备上的全部数据。Mac 上，Cue 的数据在“~/资源库/Group Containers/group.io.cueapp”中，删除 Cue 后可以一并删除这个文件夹。iCloud 中的副本可以在“设置 > Apple 账户 > iCloud > 管理储存空间”中删除。

Cue is a two-factor authentication app for Android, iOS and macOS, with a browser extension and the Cue Input Helper on the Mac. Cue does not collect any data about you.

- **No data collection**: no account, no ads, analytics, crash reporting or third-party SDKs; nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else.
- **Your data stays on your device**: the vault is stored on your device, encrypted with AES-256-GCM when you set a password.
- **Network**: Cue for Android does not request network access. Cue for iOS and macOS goes online only in two cases: if you turn on iCloud Sync, the encrypted vault is kept in your own private iCloud database, which only your Apple Account can access and the developer cannot read; and when you open the Cue Pro page, buy it or restore a purchase, the system contacts the App Store.
- **Purchases (iOS and macOS)**: Cue Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; the developer receives no payment or Apple Account information. Whether you bought it is recorded by the system on your device, and Cue checks it there.
- **Camera, photos and screen**: used only to read QR codes; images are processed on the device and neither stored nor uploaded. On a Mac, the screen is captured only when you choose Scan from Screen.
- **Backups and exports**: written only to the location you choose.
- **Keyboard and AutoFill**: the Cue keyboard and AutoFill read only Cue's own vault to provide codes; they do not read or record what you type.
- **Browser extension (Mac)**: the Cue extension for Chrome, Edge, Firefox and other browsers finds code fields on web pages. Only when you click the Cue icon, open the extension's popup or press its shortcut does it hand the current page's domain to Cue on the same Mac (through the browser's native messaging, not the network) and fill in the code Cue gives back. It reads nothing else of the page, keeps no browsing history, has no network access and stores no data.
- **Cue Input Helper (Mac)**: it receives only the codes Cue sends it and, when Cue's panel opens, reads the front app's name, its window title, the address of the page in a browser and whether a text field has focus, to decide where to type the code. This goes only to Cue on the same Mac and is neither stored nor uploaded.
- **Deleting data**: uninstalling Cue deletes all its data on the device. On a Mac, Cue's data is in ~/Library/Group Containers/group.io.cueapp, which you can delete along with the app. The copy in iCloud can be deleted under Settings > Apple Account > iCloud > Manage Storage.
