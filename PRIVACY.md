# Cue 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 13 日 · Last updated: October 13, 2026

Cue 是一款两步验证（验证码）应用，有 Android 和 iOS 两个版本。Cue 不收集你的任何数据。

- **不收集数据**：没有账号，不含广告、统计、崩溃上报或第三方 SDK，不向开发者或任何第三方发送信息。
- **数据只在你的设备上**：验证码数据库保存在你的设备上，设置了密码时用 AES-256-GCM 加密。
- **网络**：Cue for Android 不申请网络权限。Cue for iOS 只在两种情况下联网：开启“iCloud 同步”后，加密的数据库保存在你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库中，只有你的 Apple 账户能访问，开发者无法读取；打开 Cue Pro 页面、购买或恢复购买时，iOS 会连接 App Store。
- **购买（iOS）**：Cue Pro 通过 App Store 购买，付款由 Apple 处理，开发者收不到你的付款信息或 Apple 账户信息。是否已购买由 iOS 记录在你的设备上，Cue 只在本机读取。
- **相机与照片**：只用来读取二维码，画面和图片在本机处理，不保存、不上传。
- **备份与导出**：只写到你选择的位置。
- **键盘与自动填充**：Cue 键盘和自动填充只读取 Cue 的数据库来提供验证码，不读取、不记录你输入的内容。
- **删除数据**：卸载 Cue 会删除设备上的全部数据。iOS 上 iCloud 中的副本可以在“设置 > Apple 账户 > iCloud > 管理储存空间”中删除。

---

Cue is a two-factor authentication app for Android and iOS. Cue does not collect any data about you.

- **No data collection**: no account, no ads, analytics, crash reporting or third-party SDKs; nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else.
- **Your data stays on your device**: the vault is stored on your device, encrypted with AES-256-GCM when you set a password.
- **Network**: Cue for Android does not request network access. Cue for iOS goes online only in two cases: if you turn on iCloud Sync, the encrypted vault is kept in your own private iCloud database, which only your Apple Account can access and the developer cannot read; and when you open the Cue Pro page, buy it or restore a purchase, iOS contacts the App Store.
- **Purchases (iOS)**: Cue Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; the developer receives no payment or Apple Account information. Whether you bought it is recorded by iOS on your device, and Cue checks it there.
- **Camera and photos**: used only to read QR codes; images are processed on the device and neither stored nor uploaded.
- **Backups and exports**: written only to the location you choose.
- **Keyboard and AutoFill**: the Cue keyboard and AutoFill read only Cue's own vault to provide codes; they do not read or record what you type.
- **Deleting data**: uninstalling Cue deletes all its data on the device. On iOS, the copy in iCloud can be deleted under Settings > Apple Account > iCloud > Manage Storage.
